About the game

Fur Real Fashion is a pet fashion show where you don't choose your materials. You pull random accessories from a gacha, then dress the cat to fit the show's theme, and every re-roll you use to fix bad luck stresses the cat and lowers your score. The game is a five-show level, and each show adds a new rule: rerolls, treats to calm the cat, and a panel of three judges with different tastes.

Technologies

Unity 6 (UI-based 2D), C#, ScriptableObjects for data, TextMeshPro, Jira for task tracking, hand-drawn art.

My contributions

  • Game concept and core mechanic design
  • All programming: gacha, drag and drop, judging, level progression
  • Hand-drawn art: cat, accessories, judges
  • Ran playtests and implemented feedback

What playtesting changed

Playtesters felt the reroll risk strongly (5 out of 5) but didn't care about the cat's stress, felt rushed, and couldn't tell how the judges scored. In response, I added a treat system and speech bubbles so the cat's stress matters, lengthened the timers, and rebalanced the scoring so average scores are higher. I also added hints, card tooltips and a score breakdown after each show, and made sure rerolls never give you an item you're already wearing.
